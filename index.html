The problem

The nation's doorsteps have been quietly outsourced.

Doorstep milk used to be local. One dairy, a few miles away, round the corner every morning before school. Now most British households wake up to milk that's travelled from a regional depot owned by one of a handful of national operators — often hundreds of miles from the fridge it ends up in.

The milk is fine. The economics are the problem. Local dairies run better rounds, with fresher product, in glass bottles, with drivers who actually know their customers. But most households don't know you exist — because the national chains out-market you ten to one.

That's what we're here to fix.

What we do

We find your next hundred customers — one road at a time.

No retainers, no long contracts, no marketing jargon. We market your dairy to the streets around your depot, and you only pay for customers who actually sign up.

01 Street first

We pick a street

We start with one road in your delivery area. High household density, the right demographic, and close to your existing rounds so drops-per-mile stay efficient.

02 Marketing

We do the marketing

Leaflets, door-knocks, Nextdoor, local Facebook groups, a dedicated landing page. All paid for by us, all carrying your story and your bottles.

03 Signups

Signups come to you

Warm, pre-qualified, delivery preferences sorted. Your office staff don't do extra admin — they just add the new customer to the round.

04 Pay per win

You pay per customer

A simple referral fee for each confirmed signup. If we deliver nothing, you pay nothing. Once one street works, we scale to the next.

Why it works

Milk rounds live and die on route density.

You already know this. Every extra drop-per-mile improves the economics of your round. Every customer who lives two streets away from an existing one is almost pure margin.

That's why we don't scatter marketing across a whole county. We grow outward, concentrically — one road, then the adjacent roads, then the whole neighbourhood.

"You're not asking us to spread thin across a big area. You're asking us to grow outward from one dense starting point."

That's the whole model. It's how local dairies used to grow before the big chains. We just point the marketing at the right doors.
