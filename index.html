Business marketing

Getting more customers

Most small businesses are invisible to the people actively looking to buy from them. The marketing I implement fixes that:

  • A modern website built to turn visitors into enquiries
  • Quote-request & enquiry systems that capture every lead with the details you need
  • Google search presence & ads — so people actively searching for what you sell find you, not a competitor
  • Targeted outreach to the businesses most likely to need you
  • Google review management — every happy customer asked at the right moment, every review responded to, so new customers see a business they can trust

Implemented for your business specifically — not a template.

AI implementation

Running more smoothly

Where AI genuinely makes something faster or smoother, I build it in. Examples of what that can look like:

  • Every web enquiry acknowledged instantly by email — no lead left waiting
  • Quotes chased and followed up automatically, so nothing slips
  • Draft quotes generated from a description — you review, adjust and send
  • Review requests sent automatically when a job completes — and replies drafted for you to approve
  • Custom tools for the specific jobs eating your time — stock tracking, job sheets, reminders

Always with you in control — AI handles the admin, you keep the personal contact where it matters.

AI where it's useful — not everywhere. Some problems need a better website or better follow-up, not a robot. Part of what you get from me is an honest answer about which is which.
