Who we are

GrafterUK is operated by GrafterUK Ltd, a company registered in England & Wales (Company No. 17303977), registered office 71–75 Shelton Street, Covent Garden, London, WC2H 9JQ. We are registered with the Information Commissioner's Office, registration number ZC207387. For any privacy question, contact jordan@grafteruk.co.uk.

What we collect

When you contact us through the enquiry form or by email, we collect the details you provide — typically your name, email address, business, and the details of your enquiry.

Why we hold it, and our lawful basis

We use your information solely to respond to your enquiry and discuss how we might work together. Our lawful basis is legitimate interest (responding to a business enquiry you have initiated) and, where we go on to work together, performance of a contract.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with third parties for marketing. We use standard business tools (such as form handling and email hosting) to operate; those providers process data on our behalf under their own safeguards.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry information only as long as needed to deal with your enquiry and for a reasonable period afterwards, then delete it. Records relating to work we carry out are kept as long as legally required.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask to see the information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or object to our using it. Email jordan@grafteruk.co.uk and we'll respond. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Cookies & analytics

This site may use cookies and analytics tools to understand how visitors use it and to improve our service.
